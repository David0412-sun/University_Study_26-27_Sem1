SOURCE AND ATTRIBUTION NOTES
Technical citations in this revision refer to original publications and official documentation, not lecture slides.
The source mapping distinguishes works explicitly identified in the supplied material from additional primary references. Exact slide provenance is not claimed for the added sources.
Published years and journal/conference records are used instead of preprint upload dates. Page locators refer to original printed pages, not uploaded lecture PDFs.

Framework and data flow
References: Chen2022
Location/topic: pp. 20-22; Fig. 2 on p. 21
Original article explicitly identified in the supplied material.
Attribution boundary: Our diagram is a newly drawn schematic of the architecture.

Four autonomy levels
References: DoD2011
Location/topic: Section 5.4.1; Table 3, printed p. 46
Primary government report matching the four labels and definitions.
Attribution boundary: The report does not label Level 4 as Ideal. Examples are our applications of the taxonomy.

Polynomial trajectory planning
References: Mellinger2011, Richter2016
Location/topic: Minimum-snap planning; polynomial optimisation and time allocation
Both original works are explicitly identified in the supplied material.
Attribution boundary: Our A* plus polynomial combination is a proposed integration; Richter uses RRT* for its initial path.

SLAM and PnP
References: Cadena2016, Lepetit2009
Location/topic: SLAM formulation; calibrated 3D-to-2D camera pose estimation
Original review and method paper explicitly identified in the supplied material.
Attribution boundary: Known deck geometry and marker installation are proposed application choices.

Visual-inertial EKF, A* and YOLO
References: Mourikis2007, Hart1968, Redmon2016
Location/topic: Original algorithm papers
Added primary sources for the named methods; exact slide provenance is not claimed.
Attribution boundary: MSCKF supports camera/IMU estimation; GNSS updates and persistent mapping are separate integration choices.

Behaviour-tree task logic
References: Colledanchise2018
Location/topic: Sequences, fallback, conditions and retry patterns
Author-written foundational book added for task-logic support.
Attribution boundary: The mission diagram is a flowchart; its proposed logic can be implemented as a behaviour tree.

Moving-platform recovery
References: Borowczyk2017
Location/topic: Mobile-target estimation, rendezvous and relative tracking
Additional primary study supporting the moving-recovery design.
Attribution boundary: The exact touchdown taper, delivery mechanism and numerical gates remain proposed design choices.

Concrete control and communications
References: PX4, MAVLink
Location/topic: Official controller diagrams and common message definitions
Official technical documentation, accessed October 22, 2026.
Attribution boundary: PX4 mixes P and PID loops. A command acknowledgement does not establish physical delivery or receipt of a custom delivery report.
